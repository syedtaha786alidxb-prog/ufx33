Company Profile

Shown on every invoice — logo and details in the header, address/contact repeated in the footer.

Financial Year

All document numbers (Job, Invoice, Bill, Voucher) use this 2-digit year — e.g. "26" gives EXP-00001/26. Change it at year-end rollover.

Email (SMTP) Configuration

Used to send invoices directly to clients from the Invoice page. Common providers: Gmail (smtp.gmail.com, port 587), Outlook (smtp.office365.com, port 587).
