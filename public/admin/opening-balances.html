Opening Balances — One-Time Migration

Bring in your old system's financial data once, as of a cut-off date. Both uploads below post to the ledger automatically, so everything shows up in its normal place — Chart of Accounts, Ledger, Trial Balance, Receivables, and Job List.

1. Chart of Accounts — Wise (Trial Balance Upload)

Every account code in the sheet must already exist in the Chart of Accounts. If your sheet's debits and credits don't balance on their own, the difference is posted to "Opening Balance Equity" automatically.

2. Customer — Wise (Outstanding Balances)

One row per client with what they owed as of the cut-off date. Each row creates a legacy "Opening Balance" job + invoice for that client — so it appears normally under Receivables and the Client Receipt screen. New client names are created automatically.
